# Landmarks

Landmarks are NOT signature buildings.
- Signatures: free, one of each zone type, well-being / efficiency / cheaper imports.
- Landmarks: Parks & Rec → Landmarks tab. Pre-order / Treasure Hunt unlocks. You PAY to build and you PAY every month.

They sell recreation + attractiveness (tourists), not factories. They do NOT give city-wide industrial efficiency or −1% imports.

## Council law
Veto until the city is large and green.
A dog park is ₡2k/month and levels houses. A Ferris Wheel can be ₡280k–560k/month. That is a death-wave for a village budget.

Mayor may propose a landmark only if all of these are true:
- Hourly budget is solidly green
- Leisure overlay is still bad after normal parks
- Tourism/attractiveness is the actual limiter (suitcase view), not sewage or jobs
- You can afford cost + at least 12 months of upkeep without a loan

Never: landmark instead of clinic / dump / water; landmark on a red budget to "get XP"; cluster four tourist magnets in one residential pocket.
Signatures stay first. Landmarks are a late trophy if the save can carry the rent.
