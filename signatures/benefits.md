# Signature Building Benefits

Signatures are free unique zoned buildings. One of each. Tooltip in the signature menu is the live patch number.

They always do three jobs:
1. XP when placed (about 250–3,000)
2. Normal zone work — households or a company, so they still pay tax
3. A bonus — local well-being and/or a city-wide %

## Benefit types
| Bonus on the tooltip | What it does for pop / profit |
|---|---|
| +Well-being in a radius | Happiness → XP toward the next milestone |
| Households (1 to thousands) | Free residents |
| +Attractiveness % (city) | Tourism. More visitors, more commercial demand |
| −Import costs % (city) | Shops and factories pay less → profit → tax |
| +Industrial efficiency % (city) | Every factory produces better |
| +Export profits % | Extractors keep more when they sell out |
| +Ore / oil in deposits | Ground lasts longer |
| +Office efficiency / university interest / graduation | Mid-game educated jobs |
| −Crime in a radius | Office signatures only, small |
| +Entertainment / indoor recreation | Leisure overlay, late mixed/high-rises |

## By zone
**Low-density houses / mansions** — +2 to +6 well-being in 500 m–1.5 km, 1 household. Early: Rock/Pop musician, Real estate agent, Extreme athlete — 2,500 cells, +2 / 500 m, 250 XP.
**Row / medium apartments** — same well-being, but they house people. Baltar Pines / Vertigo Square: 1,000 cells, +3 / 750 m, 8 households.
**Mixed** — well-being plus +1% city attractiveness.
**Commercial** — the money ones: −1% city import costs, +attractiveness, small well-being.
**Industrial** — city-wide +1–5% industrial efficiency. Some add +10% oil or ore in the ground or +export profit.
**Office** — +office efficiency, +university interest / graduation, sometimes −crime nearby.

## Council pick order
1. Any unlocked signature with road/water/power — free XP.
2. If production is the red panel → industrial signature.
3. If shops are import-starved → commercial signature.
4. If leisure/happiness is the XP brake → residential signature on the weak well-being overlay.
5. Office signatures only after schools exist.
