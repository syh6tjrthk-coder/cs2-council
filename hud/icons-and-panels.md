# HUD Icons and Panels

| You see | Means | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| Demand bars (bottom) | Separate bars: low/row/medium/high housing, commercial, industry | Zone ONLY the bar that is full |
| City Information (next to bars) | +/- reasons: vacant homes, jobs, tax, happiness | Do not guess. Read the list. |
| Budget + red down arrow | Losing money this hour | Mayor: freeze new tiles/services |
| Budget green | Surplus | Allowed to expand |
| Info Views (top-left, ~33 overlays) | Electricity, water, traffic, pollution, resources, land value | Open matching view before build |
| Electricity view: production vs usage | kW made vs kW used | Works. Do not zone if usage > production |
| Water/sewage in the road | Pipes ride inside roads | Works. No road = no water |
| Wind/pollution arrow (industrial suitability) | Smoke blows that way | Industry downwind of homes |
| Light green streets (commercial) | Customers exist here | Commercial on those streets |
| Car icon on a building | No road access | Fix the stub road first |
| Crime symbol on buildings | Need police | Welfare, small station only |
| Leisure overlay "bad" | Parks missing, buildings will not level | Welfare, cheap park |
| Production panel (after Grand Village) | Surplus vs deficit of goods | Mayor builds the missing extractor |
| Progression panel + padlocks | Milestone and tree unlocks | Do not buy a locked building |
| Contour lines | White ≈ sea level, dark ≈ 10 m | Do not grade a mountain for a starter grid |

Hover any icon. The tooltip is law.
