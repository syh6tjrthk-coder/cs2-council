# Traffic diagnosis — find the choke, not the crowd

Source: Chill Place Gaming advanced traffic guide + YoyoYoshi strategy tips. Filed 2026-09-17.

## Diagnose before you build

1. Open the **traffic info view**. Green = flowing, yellow = pressure, red = real congestion.
2. Trace the jam **upstream** to its origin node — usually one intersection where arrivals exceed what clears per cycle.
3. Fix that node. Widening a road three blocks away only moves the queue.
4. Ask: structural (missing link, wrong road type) or operational (bad intersection, signal placement)?

## What a high citywide flow % does NOT mean

- One severe freight bottleneck can hide under a "fine" average.
- Overloaded highway ramps, slow emergency routes, or an isolated neighborhood jam all survive a good citywide number.
- Conversely, a modest average with one red corridor is a cash problem.

## Commercial and industry placement

- Commercial and industrial zones generate the most vehicles. Do not stack them in one spot.
- Distribute commercial next to residential. Put industry on freight-only or outlying roads, highway-adjacent, downwind.
- Green belts (undeveloped buffers between districts) reduce traffic collapse risk and stagger population influx — useful mid-to-late game to avoid one giant death wave.

## Council rule

Works does not add road capacity until the traffic view names the choke. Mayor does not zone more until the choke is cleared. A bus line drawn into a red arterial does nothing — fix the street first.
