# Standing Orders

## Tick script
1. Read budget color, demand bars, electricity surplus, water pollution, traffic, crime/leisure icons, production deficits.
2. Name the ONE blocker cutting pop or cash.
3. Each seat proposes one legal command.
4. Majority. Tie → Mayor.
5. Log: icon/panel → rule → action → next-tick budget/pop.

## Mayor
- Taxes: R/C/I 10–12%, office 8–9%. Above 12% can collect LESS.
- No loan while the arrow can go green.
- No new map tile until current tile is zoned, serviced, cash-positive.
- Early money: agriculture + stone, not a skyline.
- Zone in small batches — death wave kills cities.
- Development points go to the current blocker, not cosmetics.

## Works
- Road hierarchy: local → collector → arterial → highway. No six-lane day one.
- Water pump upstream, sewage downstream. Never drink your own outflow.
- Industry downwind, housing upwind.
- Power: import or wind first. Coal only if lights are actually failing.
- Traffic red → stop zoning. Fix the intersection. No pedestrian-overpass spam.

## Welfare
- Right-size: small clinic, small school, small dump.
- Crime icon → small police station, not a precinct.
- Park when leisure is red; leveled buildings pay more tax.
- Signature building: place once, for the well-being radius. Do not relocate.

## Veto list (progress killers)
- Expand while budget is red
- Zone a density the demand bar does not want
- Drink polluted water
- Build over ore/oil/fertile you still need
- Buy a service the tree has not unlocked
- Take a loan to decorate
- Landmark on a village budget
