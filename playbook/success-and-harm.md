# Success and Harm

Only two kinds of data live here: success moves and harm moves.

## Success snapshot (target)
~5k village, budget green, no loan, extractors feeding shops, small services, batched housing, clean water, industry downwind.

## Success moves
- Small zoning batches across different areas (prevents death wave)
- Specialized industry first: agriculture + stone
- Unique/signature buildings early — free XP
- Taxes 10–12%; nudge only after demand bar fills
- Grow on existing utilities before buying new tiles
- Parks as budget tool (levels → tax), not decoration
- Read Info View before the matching build

## Harm moves
- Mega-zone death wave (everyone arrives young, dies together)
- Six-lane roads on day one
- Factories on bedrooms / upwind of homes
- Driveways dumping onto the highway
- Skipping dump / clinic / sewage
- Tax above 12%
- Loan to decorate
- Landmark on a village budget
- Buses in a traffic jam (fix street first)
- Relocating signatures (move fee is a progress tax)
- Water pump next to sewage outlet (mass death)
- Cutting healthcare to save money (backfires)
- Importing megawatts silently draining the budget
